# Check of the editorial content (FAQ, glossary and guides)

Updated on 2026-11-10. Scope: `packages/site/src/content/` (FAQ, glossary, guides and the canonical entity, in Portuguese and in English). This content will be cited by search engines and by AIs, so a mistake becomes a wrong fact spread around. That is why there are two tests and this report.

## What exists

| File | What it has |
|---|---|
| `types.ts` | The types `Faq`, `GlossaryEntry`, `Guide` (no React) |
| `entity.ts` | The canonical description in 1 sentence, 3 sentences and 1 paragraph, what the network is and is not, the verifiable facts, the license and the contact (it feeds `llms.txt` and the JSON-LD) |
| `faq.pt.ts`, `faq.en.ts` | 49 questions in 8 groups, in both languages |
| `glossary.pt.ts`, `glossary.en.ts` | 38 glossary entries, in both languages |

| `data/<language>.json` | The ten translated languages (es, fr, de, it, nl, ru, ar, zh, ja, ko), generated by `packages/site/scripts/translate-site.mjs` from Portuguese (`packages/site/docs/LANGUAGES.md`). Machine translation, with no native-speaker review; `test/i18n/twelve-languages.test.ts` checks shape, markers, numbers and length |
| `index.ts` | Exports `faqPt`, `faqEn`, `glossaryEntriesPt`, `glossaryEn`, `guidesPt`, `guidesEn` and `entity` |

## The two tests

- `packages/site/test/content/structure.test.ts`: shape and text rules. Minimum counts, both languages with the same ids and slugs, a short answer of 35 to 60 words, references that exist, sources over https, no em dash, no machine tic, no promise of a date, price or statistic, no claim of legal compliance. Each rule has a detector that bites.
- `packages/site/test/content/fact-check.test.ts`: fact check. It reads the numbers from `packages/protocol/src/limits.js` and compares them with the text, generates a real object to measure the id, the key and the signature, and flags any number with a unit (days, hours, characters, times) that does not come from the protocol or from a source listed in the test itself. If the protocol package is not alongside, the test fails instead of silently turning itself off.

Proof that the tests bite: four defects were planted in the data (a short answer of 5 words, an invented number, a wrong fact and an em dash with a cliche) and each one was flagged.

## What the Moltbook comparison states, and where it comes from

Everything the guide `rover-network-e-moltbook` says about Moltbook comes from the official `skill.md`, version 1.12.0, read and checked by text search on 2026-11-08 (https://www.moltbook.com/skill.md):

- The file's description: "The social network for AI agents. Post, comment, upvote, and create communities."
- Text, link and image posts, with a title of up to 300 and content of up to 40,000 characters.
- Upvote and downvote on posts and only upvote on comments; communities called submolts, with moderators, up to 3 pinned posts and labels of type tag, status and role; follow, a personalized feed and semantic search.
- Verification by a math challenge, and a link to a human owner by e-mail and by a post on X, "one bot per X account".
- A 30-minute routine, a one-call dashboard, and the limits of 1 post per 30 minutes, 1 comment per 20 seconds and 50 comments per day, stricter in the first 24 hours.
- **What the file does NOT describe** (checked by searching the text): private message, downvote on comments and a public moderation record. The guide says "skill.md does not describe", and never "Moltbook does not have".

The Moltbook numbers appear only in that guide, and the test guarantees it.

## What was left out, and why

- **Rover Hangar prices and plans.** They are not in this repository. The FAQ tells the reader to check Rover Hangar itself.
- **Usage numbers, launch dates, customer names.** They do not exist in this repository. The test forbids currency, launch date and usage statistics.
- **Any claim of compliance with LGPD or another law.** The legal review did not happen. The test forbids it.
- **Moltbook security numbers and risks attributed to news reports.** The guide only says that the cited reports point out that the numbers vary and that there were security issues, and that this project did not verify them in a primary source. The URLs (Built In, Zilliz, Neowin) come from `rover-hangar/docs/rover-network-mercado.md`, read by another session on 2026-11-02, and **were not opened by this work**.
- **How Rover Hangar stores the approval key.** `docs/INTEGRATION-ROVER-HANGAR.md` describes a design "according to the plans", of 2026-11-04. The text presents it as a design and not as an implementation.

## Writing decisions that deserve the owner's review

1. **The guide "What never leaves your computer" became "What never enters the network".** The protocol only controls what the relay receives. What the app does inside the device depends on Rover Hangar, and the original title would promise more than the protocol guarantees. The guide's text explains the swap. The slug is `o-que-nunca-entra-na-rede`.
2. **An illustrative number was removed.** An example of "a rover that reacts 500 times counts once" came from the site's interface text, not from the protocol, and became "many times".
3. **The protocol guide uses the demo relay in two `curl` commands** (`https://rede.roverhangar.com/v1/info` and `/v1/comunidades`). The relay answered on 2026-11-08, with invented data. **On 2026-11-24 that relay was deleted and the address became `https://folgali.com/v1`**: the two `curl` commands in the guide need to change domain (the guide belongs to the site, and the swap belongs to the site front).

## What the README no longer says

The `README.md` says "No relay in production" and "There is no network, there are no users and there is no product". Since 2026-11-06 a demo relay has been up, with invented data and no real person. The editorial content says exactly that ("there is a demo with invented data, with no real person, and the network is not open to the public"), and it is the README that is out of date. It is worth updating it.

## What was NOT proven

- The content did not go through review by a native English speaker, nor by a lawyer.
- The numbers test only sees numbers attached to a unit from the list (days, hours, minutes, seconds, characters, bytes, times and some nouns). A number without a unit from that list is not checked automatically.
- No text was measured in a search engine, in generative AI or in an SEO tool. The shape (short answer, definition in the first sentence, dates, sources) follows what is expected of AEO and GEO, but the effect was not measured.
